"Do you know what great email marketing is? It’s consistent communication with your audience that brings so much value to their lives," souligne un praticien du secteur. Une communication qui crée de la valeur incite à l'attente de nouvelles informations, renforçant ainsi la fidélité et l'engagement du client.

"There is a skill to setting business goals that, once learned, will have a major impact on your ability to grow your fashion business," rappelle un expert.
